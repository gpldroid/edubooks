ABOUT EDUBOOKS

Learning resources
with a clear purpose.

EduBooks is an independent digital educational publishing project founded by Imad Limrani. It is being developed to make useful, well-structured learning materials easier to discover and use.

Our mission

Our mission is to create practical educational resources that help learners understand ideas, practise skills and make steady progress. The project is designed to serve different ages and learning stages, from early learning to independent and professional development.

What we are building

The planned library is organized around language learning, school subjects, academic study, teacher resources, early learning and lifelong learning. Materials may include digital books, workbooks, printable activities, study guides and reference resources.

Our editorial approach

  • Original or properly licensed content and visual assets.
  • Clear explanations, consistent structure and useful practice.
  • Age- and level-aware learning materials.
  • Review for accuracy, language quality and usability before publication.
  • Transparent descriptions of each resource and its intended audience.

Founder

EduBooks is developed and managed by Imad Limrani as an independent publishing initiative.

Contact

For general questions, partnerships or educational resource enquiries, a dedicated contact channel will be published before the public launch.
