# Language Foundations

**Edition:** English (EN)  
**Series:** EduBooks — Beginner Language Learning  
**Level target:** Pre-A1 to A1 (learning target, not certification)  
**Editorial status:** Working draft — not for sale or publication

## Welcome

You can begin learning a language with a clear goal, useful practice and regular review. This workbook develops practical beginner skills through short explanations, guided practice and communicative tasks.

## How to use this book

1. Study one unit at a time and read every instruction in English.
2. Say examples aloud and complete the practice before checking the answers.
3. Keep a learning record and review earlier units regularly.
4. Use fictional information in personal-profile activities if you prefer not to share personal details.

## Learning outcomes

Set realistic goals; understand the four skills; practise spelling, pronunciation, greetings, introductions, numbers, dates, personal information, classroom language and first sentences.

## Contents

1. Start Here: How Languages Work
2. The Alphabet and Spelling
3. First Sounds and Pronunciation
4. Greetings and Politeness
5. Introducing Yourself
6. Numbers, Dates and Time
7. Personal Information and Questions
8. Classroom and Learning Language
9. First Sentences
10. Integrated Beginner Project
