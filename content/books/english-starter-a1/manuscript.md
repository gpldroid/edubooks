# English Starter A1
## A Practical English Workbook for Arabic-Speaking Beginners
**EduBooks · Learn · Practice · Achieve**

> Editorial status: first manuscript draft — not yet approved for commercial publication.
> Target learner: Arabic-speaking beginner, CEFR pre-A1 to A1.
> Suggested study plan: 4 weeks, 20–30 minutes per day.

## How to use this workbook | طريقة استخدام الكتاب
Study one lesson at a time. Read the examples aloud, write your own answers, then check the answer key. Do not try to memorize every word at once. Keep a vocabulary notebook and review it every day.

ادرس درساً واحداً في كل مرة. اقرأ الأمثلة بصوت مسموع، واكتب إجاباتك بنفسك، ثم راجع الحلول. لا تحاول حفظ جميع الكلمات دفعة واحدة.

## Learning outcomes | أهداف التعلم
By the end, learners should be able to:
- introduce themselves and exchange basic personal information;
- use common classroom and everyday expressions;
- name familiar people, objects, places, food and routines;
- make simple statements and questions using *be*, *have*, present simple and *can*;
- understand and write short beginner-level messages.

# Unit 1 — Hello! | مرحباً
### Key language
- Hello. / Hi. — مرحباً
- Good morning. — صباح الخير
- Good afternoon. — مساء الخير (بعد الظهر)
- Goodbye. / See you. — إلى اللقاء
- What's your name? — ما اسمك؟
- My name is Sara. — اسمي سارة.
- Nice to meet you. — سعيد بلقائك.

### Grammar: Subject pronouns and the verb *be*
| Subject | Be | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am Ali. |
| You | are | You are kind. |
| He / She / It | is | She is a student. |
| We / They | are | They are friends. |

Contractions: I'm, you're, he's, she's, it's, we're, they're.

### Practice
1. Complete: I ___ Ahmed.
2. Complete: She ___ a teacher.
3. Choose: (They is / They are) happy.
4. Write a greeting for 9:00 a.m.
5. Introduce yourself in two sentences.

### Speaking task
A: Hello! What's your name?
B: My name is ______. What's your name?
A: I'm ______. Nice to meet you.
B: Nice to meet you, too.

# Unit 2 — Personal Information | المعلومات الشخصية
### Useful words
first name الاسم الشخصي · surname اسم العائلة · age العمر · country البلد · city المدينة · phone number رقم الهاتف · email البريد الإلكتروني · student طالب · teacher معلم

### Grammar: Questions with *be*
- What is your name? — My name is Omar.
- How old are you? — I am 18 years old.
- Where are you from? — I am from Morocco.
- Where do you live? — I live in Tangier.

### Practice
Complete the questions:
1. What ___ your name?
2. Where ___ you from?
3. How old ___ she?
4. Write three sentences about yourself. Use your real information only if you want to.

# Unit 3 — Everyday Objects and This/That | الأشياء اليومية
### Vocabulary
book كتاب · pen قلم · notebook دفتر · phone هاتف · bag حقيبة · table طاولة · chair كرسي · key مفتاح · door باب · window نافذة

### Grammar: a / an and this / that
Use **a** before a consonant sound: a book, a pen.
Use **an** before a vowel sound: an apple, an orange.
- This is a book. (near)
- That is a window. (far)
- Is this your phone? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.

### Practice
Choose a or an:
1. ___ apple
2. ___ notebook
3. ___ orange
4. ___ umbrella

Write two sentences about objects near you.

# Unit 4 — Family and People | العائلة والأشخاص
### Vocabulary
mother أم · father أب · sister أخت · brother أخ · parents الوالدان · friend صديق · family عائلة · child طفل · children أطفال

### Grammar: Possessive adjectives and *have*
my, your, his, her, our, their
- This is my sister.
- His name is Adam.
- Her family is small.
- I have two brothers.
- She has one sister.

### Practice
1. Sara is my friend. ___ name is Sara.
2. I have a brother. ___ brother is kind.
3. He ___ two sisters. (have / has)
4. Write three simple sentences about a family (real or imaginary).

# Unit 5 — Daily Routines | الروتين اليومي
### Vocabulary
wake up يستيقظ · get dressed يرتدي ملابسه · have breakfast يتناول الفطور · go to school يذهب إلى المدرسة · study يدرس · work يعمل · go home يعود إلى المنزل · sleep ينام

### Grammar: Present simple
Use the base verb with I/you/we/they:
- I study English.
- They work in a school.

Add **-s/-es** with he/she/it:
- She studies English.
- He goes to work.

Questions: Do you study? Does she work?
Negative: I don't work. He doesn't work.

### Practice
1. He (study / studies) English.
2. They (go / goes) to school.
3. ___ you work? (Do / Does)
4. She ___ like coffee. (don't / doesn't)
5. Write four sentences about your day.

# Unit 6 — Food and Drinks | الطعام والشراب
### Vocabulary
bread خبز · water ماء · milk حليب · tea شاي · coffee قهوة · rice أرز · chicken دجاج · apple تفاحة · banana موزة · vegetables خضروات

### Useful language
- I'd like some water, please.
- Can I have a coffee?
- I like apples.
- I don't like tea.
- How much is it?

### Grammar: Countable and uncountable nouns
Countable: an apple, two apples, a sandwich.
Uncountable: water, milk, rice.
Use **some** in many positive requests/statements: some water, some apples.
Use **any** in many questions and negatives: Do you have any tea? I don't have any milk.

### Practice
1. Choose: (a / some) water.
2. Choose: (an / some) apples.
3. Complete: I'd like ___ bread, please.
4. Write a two-line café conversation.

# Unit 7 — Places and Directions | الأماكن والاتجاهات
### Vocabulary
school مدرسة · hospital مستشفى · station محطة · market سوق · bank بنك · street شارع · left يسار · right يمين · straight ahead إلى الأمام · near قريب

### Useful language
- Where is the bank?
- Go straight ahead.
- Turn left at the street.
- The station is next to the market.
- It is near the school.

### Grammar: There is / There are
- There is a bank near here.
- There are two shops on this street.
- Is there a hospital? Yes, there is.
- Are there any cafés? No, there aren't.

### Practice
1. ___ a school near my house. (There is / There are)
2. ___ two books on the table. (There is / There are)
3. Write directions from your home to a familiar place (use an imaginary route if preferred).

# Unit 8 — Abilities, Time and Plans | القدرات والوقت والخطط
### Grammar: Can / can't
- I can swim.
- She can speak Arabic.
- He can't drive.
- Can you help me? Yes, I can.

### Telling the time
It's seven o'clock. — الساعة السابعة.
It's half past eight. — الثامنة والنصف.
It's quarter past nine. — التاسعة والربع.
It's quarter to ten. — العاشرة إلا ربعاً.

### Simple plans
- What time is the class? It is at ten.
- See you tomorrow.
- I want to learn English.
- I would like to practise every day.

### Practice
1. Complete: I ___ speak Arabic. (can / cans)
2. Make a question with *can* and *you*.
3. Write the time 7:30 in words.
4. Write a short message inviting a friend to study English.

# Review Test | اختبار المراجعة
1. I ___ from Morocco. (am / is)
2. She ___ a student. (are / is)
3. He ___ English every day. (study / studies)
4. ___ you like tea? (Do / Does)
5. There ___ two chairs. (is / are)
6. I ___ swim. (can / cans)
7. Choose: (a / an) orange.
8. Write one question asking someone's name.
9. Write three sentences describing your daily routine.
10. Write a short self-introduction (30–40 words).
