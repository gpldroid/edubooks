## Publication note
This is a self-study vocabulary workbook for familiar everyday situations. Vocabulary is selected for practical learning and is not presented as a statistically ranked frequency list. Regional alternatives are noted where useful. This is an independent resource and is not an official examination or certification.

## Welcome
Welcome. This workbook teaches useful vocabulary through everyday situations. Learn complete expressions, practise aloud and revisit words from memory.

## How to use this book
Read the situation. Study each expression in context. Complete the practice without looking back, then check the answer key. Review after one day, three days, one week and two weeks.

## Learning outcomes
By the end, you can discuss people, home, food, shopping, routines, places, travel, work, basic health needs, weather and preferences; use common word partnerships; and plan your own vocabulary review.

# Unit 1 — People, Family and Relationships

**Can-do:** Introduce people, explain family relationships and describe personality.

## Discover
This is my cousin Maya. She lives near our grandparents. She is friendly and enjoys meeting people.

## Vocabulary in context
- relative
- cousin
- neighbour
- colleague
- partner
- child
- grandparents
- friendly
- helpful
- get on well with

## Language focus
Use possessives (my, his, their) and the present simple for facts. Say “get on well with someone”.

## Guided practice
1) My mother's brother is my ____. 2) Maya ____ (live) near us. 3) Write two sentences about two people.

## Answer check
uncle; lives; answers vary

## Communicate
Introduce two people, their relationship to you and one quality each.

# Unit 12 — Build Your Vocabulary System

**Can-do:** Use collocations, word families and spaced retrieval.

## Discover

## Vocabulary in context

- do homework
- take a photo
- pay attention
- word family
- cognate
- false friend
- retrieve

## Language focus
Learn words in chunks. Similar spelling does not guarantee the same meaning. Review from memory at increasing intervals.

## Guided practice

# Integrated Project — Vocabulary Portfolio

Create a five-part portfolio: people, home, food, daily routine and leisure. For each theme, record eight words, three common expressions, three original sentences and one short dialogue. Add a review date for every new item.

## Assessment criteria
- Relevance and range of vocabulary.
- Correct use of articles, agreement and verb forms.
- Natural word combinations.
- Clear communication in the target language.
- A realistic review plan.

# Final Review Test

1. What word describes your mother's brother?
2. Complete: There ____ two chairs in the room.
3. Complete the quantity phrase: a ____ of water.
4. Complete the expression: try it ____.
5. Write one sentence about your daily routine.
6. Give two clear directions to a public place.
7. Write one polite question to ask at a hotel reception.
8. Name one task you do at work or in a class.
9. Write one sentence describing a simple symptom.
10. Describe the weather today in one sentence.
11. State a preference and give one reason.
12. Complete the collocation: do ____.

# Glossary
- **Collocation:** a combination of words that commonly occurs together.
- **Cognate:** a word related in form and meaning to a word in another language.
- **False friend:** a similar-looking word with a different meaning or use.
- **Word family:** words related by form and meaning but used in different grammatical roles.
- **Retrieval practice:** recalling information without looking at the source.
- **Spaced review:** revisiting information at increasing intervals.
