# EduBooks — Series 01: Detailed Contents

**Learning languages:** English, French, Spanish  
**Explanation languages:** English, French, Spanish  
**Editorial rule:** Every book is written for learners of all three languages. Instructions, explanations, examples and answer keys are provided in the three languages. No Arabic edition is planned.

## Shared instructional architecture

Each unit follows this sequence:
1. **Can-do outcomes** — observable skills linked to CEFR.
2. **Discover** — a short context, model or diagnostic task.
3. **Understand** — concise explanations in English, French and Spanish.
4. **Compare** — parallel examples that preserve communicative meaning, not literal word order.
5. **Notice pronunciation** — sound, stress, rhythm and spelling guidance.
6. **Build vocabulary** — useful chunks, not isolated translations only.
7. **Guided practice** — recognition and controlled production.
8. **Communicate** — a realistic speaking or writing task.
9. **Review** — retrieval practice and self-assessment.
10. **Answer key** — answers plus explanations where more than one form may be valid.

A typical unit is designed for 45–75 minutes. Learners may study one language at a time or compare all three. English, French and Spanish examples must be checked independently by competent language reviewers.

## Cross-series editorial and production controls

- Each language version must be idiomatic and independently reviewed; parallel examples must share intent, not necessarily structure.
- Explain grammar in the three target languages, with language-specific terminology kept consistent.
- Mark formal/informal usage and regional variants where they affect meaning.
- All dialogues, reading passages, exercises and answer keys must be original or properly licensed.
- Do not claim CEFR certification or endorsement. CEFR levels are instructional targets only.
- Every exercise must have a verified answer key; open-ended tasks must include model answers or a clear rubric.
- Maintain a source and permissions log for every third-party asset.
- Final PDFs must be searchable, printable, bookmarked and checked on mobile and desktop.
- Product pages and checkout links remain unpublished until content review, language review, PDF quality assurance, pricing and payout checks are complete.
