# Archived: Store operations are out of scope

**Status:** Retired by owner decision on 2026-11-22.

EduBooks will not sell books. The approved distribution model is a free library on the EduBooks website, with an in-browser PDF preview and direct PDF download.

- Do not create, edit, attach files to, or publish Gumroad products for this project.
- Do not configure PayPal, checkout, payment links, prices, subscriptions or paid delivery.
- Do not use historical product drafts or their pricing data in the website.
- Do not delete or change external seller-account records as part of repository maintenance; they are outside this repository and are not part of the active workflow.

This file is retained only so future maintainers understand why earlier store setup notes and product references are obsolete. The active specification is [SITE-ONLY-PUBLISHING-MODEL.md](SITE-ONLY-PUBLISHING-MODEL.md).
