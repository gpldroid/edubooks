# EduBooks — Multilingual Learning Series 01
## Editorial and authoring blueprint

**Languages:** English, French, Spanish  
**Explanation language:** The three target languages only. No Arabic edition or Arabic interface.  
**Audience:** Global self-directed learners; beginner through lower-intermediate.  
**Delivery:** Searchable, printable PDF workbooks.  
**Pedagogy:** Parallel multilingual learning, contrastive examples, communicative tasks, spaced review and answer keys.

## Series concept

A three-language learning library in which English, French and Spanish are treated as equal target languages. Each lesson presents a communicative objective, then parallel examples in all three languages, concise language-specific notes, pronunciation guidance, controlled practice and a real-world task. Avoid word-for-word translation where grammar or idiom differs. Label regional variants where relevant and use consistent international vocabulary.

Each book should contain:
- learning outcomes and a short diagnostic;
- modular lessons with estimated study time;
- side-by-side English / French / Spanish examples;
- pronunciation and spelling notes appropriate to each language;
- vocabulary banks and grammar reference tables;
- guided practice, communicative tasks and self-checks;
- cumulative review after every 3–4 units;
- full answer key with explanations;
- glossary and references.

## Book 1 — Language Foundations
**Working subtitle:** Start communicating in English, French and Spanish  
**Target:** absolute beginners / CEFR pre-A1 to A1  
**Outcome:** introduce oneself, understand basic classroom language, recognize core sounds and produce simple statements.

1. How to use this book; language-learning habits; sound-symbol awareness.
2. Alphabet, letter names and spelling names / email addresses.
3. Vowels, consonants, stress and basic pronunciation differences.
4. Greetings, leave-taking and polite expressions.
5. Introducing yourself: name, origin, languages and occupation.
6. Numbers, dates, days, months and time.
7. Personal information and simple questions.
8. Classroom instructions and essential learning phrases.
9. Basic sentence patterns: statements, questions and negation.
10. Review project: a short personal profile in all three languages.

Assessments: alphabet and sound recognition, spelling, information exchange, short written profile, integrated A1 checkpoint.

4. Clothing, colours, sizes and shopping.

## Book 3 — Grammar Made Clear
**Target:** A1–A2 with selected B1 bridges  
**Outcome:** build accurate simple and connected sentences in the three languages.

1. Parts of speech and sentence order.
2. Personal and demonstrative pronouns.
3. Nouns, gender, number and articles (language-specific comparison).
4. Adjectives, agreement and comparison.
5. Present forms and high-frequency verbs.
6. Negation and question formation.
7. Possession, quantity and articles/quantifiers.
8. Prepositions of place, time and movement.
9. Past-time foundations and common past forms.
10. Future plans and intentions.
11. Modal expressions, requests and advice.
12. Connectors and compound sentences.
13. Common learner errors and contrastive grammar.
Appendices: conjugation reference, irregular verb lists and punctuation.

1. Meeting people and small talk.

4. Shopping, prices, returns and services.
5. Directions, public transport and travel problems.
6. Hotels, bookings and check-in.

**Target:** A2–B1  
**Outcome:** understand everyday texts and create coherent practical writing.

1. Reading strategies: skimming, scanning and context clues.
2. Forms, notices, signs and instructions.
3. Emails and personal messages.
4. Descriptions of people, places and experiences.
5. Narrating past events.
6. Giving instructions and explaining processes.

## Book 6 — Exercises & Assessment
**Target:** Pre-A1 through B1 review  
**Outcome:** diagnose progress and demonstrate integrated language skills.

1. Entry diagnostic and learning plan.
2. Foundations and pronunciation checks.
3. Vocabulary retrieval and contextual use.
4. Grammar accuracy and sentence building.
5. Reading comprehension by level.
6. Guided and independent writing.
7. Functional dialogue and speaking prompts.
8. Mixed-skill A1 mock assessment.
9. A2 progress assessment.
10. B1 bridge assessment.
11. Error analysis and remediation plans.

Include answer keys, scoring rubrics, self-assessment grids and clear explanations. Speaking tasks use prompts and rubrics, not unsupported claims of automated scoring.

## Production standards and QA

- Write original content; document sources and licenses for any third-party material.
- Use neutral international English, standard contemporary French and standard contemporary Spanish; flag meaningful regional differences.
- Have a qualified reviewer for each target language review idiom, grammar and answer keys.
- Ensure examples are equivalent in communicative intent, not forced literal translations.
- Test all exercises against the answer key; no ambiguous multiple-choice questions.
- Use consistent CEFR labels but do not claim official certification or endorsement.
- Make PDF text selectable/searchable, use accessible contrast, bookmarks and tagged headings where tooling permits.
- Keep the six books as separate products and also prepare a complete-series bundle after all six pass review.

## Recommended order

Author Book 1 first and use it to validate the trilingual lesson template. Freeze the template after review, then produce Books 2–6. Do not publish or create checkout links until manuscript, language review, PDF QA, cover rights, pricing and seller payout readiness are approved.
