| English usage | “Good afternoon” is glossed as “مساء الخير (بعد الظهر)”. This may be acceptable in some contexts but needs a consistent Arabic language-editor decision. | Confirm the intended register and regional Arabic equivalent. |
| Grammar scope | Unit 6 introduces *some* and *any* with simplified rules. | Add a short note that these are common patterns, not absolute rules; verify examples and learner level. |
| Grammar scope | Unit 8 combines ability, clock time and simple plans. | Check sequencing and cognitive load for pre-A1/A1 learners. |
