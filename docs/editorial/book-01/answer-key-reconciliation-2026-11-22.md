# Book 01 — answer-key reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-11-22  
**Scope:** All ten units in the independent English, French and Spanish first-draft manuscripts.  
**Method:** Cross-check every closed-answer practice prompt against its corresponding answer-key entry; check that open speaking/writing tasks have criteria rather than one compulsory personal answer.

## Result

- English: Units 1–10 cross-checked.
- French: Unités 1–10 cross-checked.
- Spanish: Unidades 1–10 cross-checked.
- Open-ended profile and communication tasks are appropriately treated as variable responses with stated assessment criteria; fictional details are allowed and private information is not required.
- One definite mismatch was identified in French Unit 3, item 3. The prompt asks why learners should listen to French word groups, but the former key listed syllable fragments. The key now gives the relevant explanation about connected speech and natural phrase rhythm.

## Technical proof

The correction was merged in PR #26. The regenerated French PDF from workflow [#45035163849](https://github.com/gpldroid/edubooks/actions/runs/45035163849) was downloaded and checked:
- 11 A4 pages.
- Searchable/selectable text; 4,661 extracted words.
- DejaVu Sans regular, bold and italic fonts embedded.
- The corrected answer appears in the PDF answer key: “Il faut écouter les groupes de mots pour percevoir les enchaînements et le rythme naturel de la phrase.”

The English and Spanish PDFs were also regenerated successfully in the same three-language workflow. Their manuscript content was unchanged in this correction.

## Remaining limits

This is an answer-to-prompt consistency pass, not a professional proofread. It does not certify regional naturalness, CEFR alignment, pedagogical sequencing, accessibility, rights or complete page-by-page typography. Qualified independent reviewers must still record their sign-off before any edition is marked approved.
