## 3. Editorial finding and correction

The French Unit 3 answer key contained a definite mismatch: answer 3 listed syllable-stress fragments (`ca ; mi ; lé`) even though the corresponding question asks why learners should listen to French word groups rather than isolate every word. PR #26 corrected the French source and the updated PDF was rebuilt successfully in workflow [#45035163849](https://github.com/gpldroid/edubooks/actions/runs/45035163849). The corrected sentence was confirmed in the regenerated PDF.

This is a targeted correction, not a claim that the manuscripts have been professionally proofread. A first exercise-to-answer cross-check of all ten units in EN/FR/ES has now been recorded in [the answer-key reconciliation report](answer-key-reconciliation-2026-11-22.md). No other obvious closed-answer mismatch was identified in that pass; qualified language and pedagogical review remain open.

## 5. Release decision

**Not approved for commercial release.** The corrected French proof is an internal review artifact. The repository already contained earlier PDFs in `public/books/` before this review; a post-merge workflow refreshed those files automatically, so the previous public file blobs were restored in commit `20d1c51572e224a75397982ab4933e694a8fb9c7`. The PDF workflow is being changed to upload internal artifacts only and never commit to the public library. The following gates remain open for each language edition:

1. Qualified target-language editing and recorded reviewer sign-off.
2. Pedagogical review of learning progression and suitability for the stated Pre-A1/A1 learning target.
3. Complete exercise/answer-key reconciliation, including open-response marking guidance.
4. Rights, attribution and licence review.
5. PDF accessibility and full-resolution visual proof.
6. Final metadata, cover, preview, checksum and independent release checklist.

The public catalogue must remain empty until approved preview assets and edition metadata exist. Keep all Gumroad drafts unpublished and do not attach these review artifacts.
