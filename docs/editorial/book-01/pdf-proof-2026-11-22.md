## 3. Editorial finding and correction

The French Unit 3 answer key contained a definite mismatch: answer 3 listed syllable-stress fragments (`ca ; mi ; lé`) even though the corresponding question asks why learners should listen to French word groups rather than isolate every word. The mismatch was corrected in the French source manuscript on the review branch. The PDF must be rebuilt and rechecked before this correction is considered present in the proof artifact.

This is a targeted correction, not a claim that the entire answer key or the French manuscript has been professionally proofread. English, French and Spanish answer keys still require a complete exercise-by-exercise reconciliation.
