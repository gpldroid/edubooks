Privacy notice.

How this initial EduBooks website handles information.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Current website operation

This launch website is an informational static website. It does not provide user accounts, checkout, product downloads, newsletter subscriptions or an on-site contact form. Do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.

2. Information collected by this website

EduBooks does not intentionally collect personal information through forms on this website. The hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP address, request metadata and security logs, to deliver and protect the website. Please consult the hosting provider's privacy documentation for its processing practices.

3. External services

This website may load fonts from Google Fonts. When those resources are requested, the relevant provider may receive technical connection information. External links are governed by the privacy policies of their respective services.

4. Future purchases

If digital products are introduced, checkout and payment processing will be handled by a third-party provider. The provider's privacy notice and checkout terms will apply to information submitted during a purchase. This notice will be updated before sales begin.

5. Children's privacy

The planned educational resources may be suitable for children, but this website is not designed to collect personal information directly from children.

6. Contact and updates

This notice must be reviewed and updated before adding analytics, forms, accounts, mailing lists or payment features. For privacy questions, use the contact information published on the About page.
