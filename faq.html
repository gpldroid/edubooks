questions.

Helpful information about the EduBooks project and the resources planned for the library.

What is EduBooks?

EduBooks is an independent digital educational publishing project. The planned library will include digital books, workbooks, printable learning activities and study guides.

Who are the resources for?

The project is intended to serve children, school learners, university students, adult learners and educators. Each published product will state its intended age group and learning level.

Which languages will be available?

English and Arabic are the initial language priorities. Additional languages may be introduced as the library grows.

Are products available to buy now?

No. The library and its first collections are being prepared. No product is currently offered for sale through this website.

How will digital products be delivered?

When the store opens, eligible digital purchases will be delivered through the selected checkout and digital delivery provider. Product-specific file formats and access instructions will be shown before purchase.

Can teachers or organizations contact EduBooks?

Yes. For enquiries about educational resources or possible collaborations, use the contact details on the About page.
